About Jeffrey Remedies

Your Strategic Third-Party Manufacturing & Pharma Partner

Jeffrey Remedies, established in 2021 under the leadership of proprietor Mr. Khusroo Jafri, is a third-party manufacturing company, supplier, and trading enterprise with headquarters in Prayagraj and corporate office in New Delhi.
