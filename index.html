About Jeffrey Remedies

Dedicated to Ethical Pharmaceutical Marketing & Superior Formulations

Founded with a vision to bridge the gap between advanced medical science and patient accessibility, **Jeffrey Remedies** stands as a trusted name in the pharmaceutical sector. Headquartered in Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh, we specialize in high-grade pharmaceutical formulations ranging from tablets and capsules to pediatric syrups and injectables.

Our products are manufactured in state-of-the-art third-party manufacturing facilities that strictly adhere to **WHO-GMP** and **ISO certification** standards. We empower medical professionals with safe, reliable, and affordable therapeutics.
